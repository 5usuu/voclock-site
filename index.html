Small moments, real progress

A few new words a day, asked again and again until you know them, with the most common words first.

Words on your lock screen

Answer a word to unlock. Stuck? Letter hints help, and your emergency PIN always gets you in.

Smart review

Words come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 days and more, right before you'd forget them.

Hear every word

Tap the speaker to hear how a word sounds, using your phone's built-in voice.

News in your new language

Read today's news from Wikipedia with every word you know swapped into the language you're learning.

Home-screen widget

Flip through today's words right on your home screen, with example sentences and a new word every time you unlock. Part of VocLock Pro.

Private by design

No account needed to learn. If you make one, only your progress goes into it, and you can delete it any time. Read the privacy policy.
