The VocLock app

Learn new words when you unlock your phone.

You unlock your phone dozens of times a day. VocLock turns some of those moments into a quick word from the language you're learning (at most one every ten minutes), so the words stick without setting time aside.

Small moments, real progress

A few new words a day, asked again and again until you know them, with the most common words first. Around the lock screen there is a whole app: games, ranks, friends, stories and more. How VocLock compares to Duolingo and Anki.

Words on your lock screen

Answer a word to unlock. Stuck? Letter hints help, and your emergency PIN always gets you in.

You stay in control

At most one word every ten minutes, and none on Do Not Disturb. In a hurry? Your emergency PIN skips the word. Lock-screen words can be switched off in Settings any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Smart review

Words come back after 1, 3, 7, 14 days and more, right before you'd forget them.

Hear every word

Tap the speaker to hear how a word sounds, using your phone's built-in voice.

News in your new language

Read today's news from Wikipedia with every word you know swapped into the language you're learning.

Home-screen widgets

Flip through today's words right on your home screen, with example sentences and a new word each time you come back to your phone. There are also widgets for your streak, your rank and your learning partner. Part of VocLock Pro.

An otter that learns with you

A little otter with a cup of tea cheers when you get a word right and gets grumpy when you skip. Dress it up with a bow, a top hat, glasses or a sun hat.

Streaks, ranks and exams

Keep a daily streak, climb through ranks by passing a short exam for each one, and collect more than 50 achievements along the way.

Friends and a learning partner

Add friends to see each other's progress. Pair up with one partner for a shared streak, and send a nudge when the other one hasn't learned yet today.

Six word games

Match pairs, a crossword, a word search, building sentences, shuffled letters and an otter catch game, all played with the words you have already met, in three difficulty levels.

Daily challenge and weekly quiz

A short challenge every day, free practice whenever you like, and a weekly quiz with example sentences that shows what really stuck.

A picture for the word

Hundreds of everyday words come with a small picture drawn for VocLock, so you link the new word to the thing itself and not only to its translation.

New alphabets, step by step

Japanese starts with hiragana and katakana and has its own kanji practice. Russian and Korean begin with their alphabets, and Chinese shows pinyin.

Your own words

Add any word yourself, or paste a whole list copied from Quizlet, Anki or a spreadsheet. Your words are asked and repeated like all the others.

Stories and a chat partner

"Tea" is a chat story to read along, one episode a day. With Pro you can text with the otter in the language you're learning; it answers at your level. The chat partner is an AI.

Mini courses with grammar

Short units that explain a bit of grammar and practise it right away. The first course is French for German speakers; more languages are in the works.

Reel brake

Optional on Android: after every few TikToks, Reels or Shorts, VocLock asks one word before you scroll on.

Free, or VocLock Pro

Learning is free, with ads. Pro removes the ads and adds more new words a day, every Tea episode, the widgets, streak freezes and the chat partner. Everyone gets a free Pro test week that ends by itself.

Private by design

No account needed to learn. If you make one, only your progress goes into it (plus what you choose to share with friends), and you can delete it any time. Read the privacy policy.
