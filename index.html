How it works

Learning a language usually needs time you don't have. VocLock uses the seconds you already spend on your phone.

  1. Unlock your phone

    A word from your course shows up, at most one every ten minutes and never on Do Not Disturb.

  2. Answer in a few seconds

    Type the word, or take a letter hint. Your emergency PIN always gets you in.

  3. It comes back at the right time

    Words you know wait longer, words you missed come back sooner, until they stick.

Small moments, real progress

Show more

    Tap the speaker to hear how a word sounds, using your phone's built-in voice.

    Hundreds of everyday words come with a small picture drawn for VocLock, so the word links to the thing.
