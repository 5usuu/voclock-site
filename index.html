How it works

Learning a language usually needs time you don't have. VocLock uses the seconds you already spend on your phone.

  1. Unlock your phone

    A word from your course shows up, at most one every ten minutes and never on Do Not Disturb.

  2. Answer in a few seconds

    Type the word, or take a letter hint. Your emergency PIN always gets you in.

  3. It comes back at the right time

    Words you know wait longer, words you missed come back sooner, until they stick.

Small moments, real progress

A few new words a day, asked again until you know them, with the most common words first. How VocLock compares to Duolingo and Anki.

Around the lock screen

A whole app for the minutes you do have.

A short challenge every day, free practice whenever you like, and a weekly quiz with example sentences that shows what really stuck. Read "Tea", a chat story with one episode a day, or with Pro text with the otter in your new language; it answers at your level. The chat partner is an AI.

  • Six word games in three levels, with the words you've already met
  • 20 A1 and 20 A2 mini courses with grammar
  • Today's news from Wikipedia, with the words you know swapped in

Remember what you learn

Words that stay. Progress you can see.

And there's more

  • You stay in control

    Your emergency PIN always gets you in, and lock-screen words can be switched off any time.

  • Reel brake

    Optional on Android: after every few TikToks, Reels or Shorts, VocLock asks one word before you scroll on.

  • Hear every word

    Tap the speaker to hear how a word sounds, using your phone's built-in voice.

  • A picture for the word

    Hundreds of everyday words come with a small picture drawn for VocLock, so the word links to the thing.

  • New alphabets, step by step

    Japanese starts with hiragana and katakana and has its own kanji practice. Russian and Korean begin with their alphabets, and Chinese shows pinyin.

  • Your own words

    Add words yourself, paste a list from Quizlet, Anki or a spreadsheet, or import CSV and Anki .apkg files.

  • Home-screen widgets

    Today's words with example sentences and your learning partner at a glance (both Pro). The rank widget is free.

  • Free, or VocLock Pro

    Learning is free, with ads. Pro removes the ads and adds more new words a day, every Tea episode, the Pro widgets, streak freezes and the chat partner. Everyone gets a free Pro test week that ends by itself.

  • Private by design

    No account needed to learn. If you make one, only your progress goes into it (plus what you share with friends), and you can delete it any time. Privacy policy.
