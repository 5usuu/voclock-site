How it works

Learning a language usually needs time you don't have. VocLock uses the seconds you already spend on your phone.

  1. Unlock your phone

    A word from your course shows up, at most one every ten minutes and never on Do Not Disturb.

  2. Answer in a few seconds

    Type the word, or take a letter hint. Your emergency PIN always gets you in.

  3. It comes back at the right time

    Words you know wait longer, words you missed come back sooner, until they stick.

Small moments, real progress

A few new words a day, asked again and again until you know them, with the most common words first. Around the lock screen there is a whole app: games, ranks, friends, stories and more. How VocLock compares to Duolingo and Anki.

Learn while you unlock

Words on your lock screen

Answer a word to unlock. Stuck? Letter hints help, and your emergency PIN always gets you in.

You stay in control

At most one word every ten minutes, none on Do Not Disturb, and your emergency PIN always skips it. Lock-screen words can be switched off any time.

Reel brake

Optional on Android: after every few TikToks, Reels or Shorts, VocLock asks one word before you scroll on.

Remember what you learn

Smart review

Words come back at adaptive intervals (FSRS), timed to when you're about to forget them. Tell the app if an answer was easy or hard and the next date follows.

Hear every word

Tap the speaker to hear how a word sounds, using your phone's built-in voice.

A picture for the word

Hundreds of everyday words come with a small picture drawn for VocLock, so the word links to the thing, not just to its translation.

New alphabets, step by step

Japanese starts with hiragana and katakana and has its own kanji practice. Russian and Korean begin with their alphabets, and Chinese shows pinyin.

Your own words

Add words yourself, paste a list from Quizlet, Anki or a spreadsheet, or import CSV and Anki .apkg files. They're asked like all the others.

Home-screen widgets

Today's words with example sentences right on your home screen, and your learning partner at a glance (both Pro). The rank widget is free.

Read, play and talk

Mini courses with grammar

Short units that explain a bit of grammar and practise it right away: 20 A1 and 20 A2 courses. Japanese, Korean and Chinese can show the reading (kana, romanization or pinyin).

News in your new language

Read today's news from Wikipedia with every word you know swapped into the language you're learning.

Stories and a chat partner

"Tea" is a chat story to read along, one episode a day. With Pro, text with the otter in your new language; it answers at your level. The chat partner is an AI.

Six word games

Match pairs, crossword, word search, sentence building, shuffled letters and otter catch, with the words you've already met, in three levels.

Daily challenge and weekly quiz

A short challenge every day, free practice whenever you like, and a weekly quiz with example sentences that shows what really stuck.

Keep going

An otter that learns with you

A little otter with a cup of tea cheers when you get a word right and gets grumpy when you skip. Dress it up with a bow, a top hat, glasses or a sun hat.

Streaks, ranks and exams

Keep a daily streak, climb through ranks by passing a short exam for each one, and collect more than 50 achievements along the way.

Friends and a learning partner

See your friends' progress, pair up with one partner for a shared streak, and nudge them when they haven't learned yet today.

Fair and private

Free, or VocLock Pro

Learning is free, with ads. Pro removes the ads and adds more new words a day, every Tea episode, the Pro widgets, streak freezes and the chat partner. Everyone gets a free Pro test week that ends by itself.

Private by design

No account needed to learn. If you make one, only your progress goes into it (plus what you choose to share with friends), and you can delete it any time. Read the privacy policy.
