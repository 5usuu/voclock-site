VocLock compared to Duolingo and Anki

Written by the maker of VocLock, as fairly as he can. Last updated November 2026.

In short

  • VocLock brings the words to you: one word when you unlock your phone. You don't need to open an app or find time. Best for building and keeping vocabulary without a study routine.
  • Duolingo is a full course you sit down for: lessons with grammar, listening and speaking, in many languages. Best if you want to be led through a language from zero.
  • Anki is a flashcard tool you build yourself: any subject, any card layout, very fine control over review. Best if you like to set things up your own way.

Where VocLock is stronger

  • You can't forget to study. The word shows up when you pick up your phone. No reminder to ignore, no app to open.
  • It asks little of you. One word, a few seconds, and an emergency PIN when you're in a hurry.
  • Ready from the first minute. The word lists are already there, sorted by how often words are used, with example sentences and pictures.
  • It doesn't need an account or a connection. Without an account, everything stays on your phone.

Where the others are stronger

  • Duolingo teaches whole sentences, grammar, listening and speaking in long, guided courses, and offers far more languages. VocLock's grammar courses are only beginning.
  • Anki can hold anything, from medicine to music theory, with your own card layouts, images and audio, and review settings you can tune in detail. VocLock is made for words of a language and keeps the settings simple.

Can VocLock replace them?

For vocabulary: yes. VocLock introduces new words, repeats them until they stick, takes your own words and lists, and adds games, a weekly quiz and news to read. You don't need a second app for words.

For a whole language, with grammar and speaking practice: not yet in every course. VocLock is growing in that direction, with more mini courses and listening and speaking exercises on the way. Until then it works well next to a course, a class or a tutor: they explain, VocLock makes sure the words are there when you need them.

Who VocLock is for

  • You've tried learning apps and stopped opening them after a few weeks.
  • You're in a class or with a tutor and the vocabulary doesn't stick.
  • You want to pick up a language slowly, in the background, without a study plan.
  • You want to spend less time scrolling and get something out of picking up your phone.
